MYDEA / PERSONAL MEDIA WORKSPACEmacOS

Discover, organize, play.All in one place.

Build my own media universe

Mydea is a privacy-first personal media workspace. It connects mainstream media information and data platforms with your own media services and shared files, reorganizing scattered media worlds into the shape you want.

Explore Mydea

The platform wants your viewing habits and records.Mydea only cares about how you feel.

The same work may appear in charts, Plex, drives, and Trakt. Your data is controlled by you. Mydea only connects, does not collect information, and does not even have its own server system.
